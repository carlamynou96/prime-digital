BEFORE YOU BOOK

Clear answers. Clear costs.

Does R500 buy first-page ranking?

R500 covers a review of one existing page, basic search recommendations, a keyword shortlist and a next-step plan. It does not buy an ad placement or guarantee an organic ranking.

What is separate from the R1,500 setup?

Advertising spend, daily ads support, domain registration and renewal are separate. Live hosting and two domain emails are offered at R150/month; availability and provider terms are confirmed in the written proposal.

Does R750 guarantee two hours on Google’s first page?

No. R750 is the service fee for support around an agreed advertising window of at least two hours. Google ad spend is separate. Scheduling allows the campaign to participate during that window; it does not guarantee continuous delivery, first-page placement, top position or a number of clients.

Who owns my Google Ads account?

Your campaign is set up in your own account. You approve the service scope and advertising budget before launch. Never send your Google password by email; access arrangements are agreed separately.

Are ads, enquiries or sales guaranteed?

No. Search placement and customer response vary. The starter package provides defined setup work. Measurement, ongoing optimisation and additional work must be agreed separately.

How do I request a quote?

Email your business name, service, customer location, existing website (if any), chosen package and preferred timeline. Carla will confirm the scope and separate costs before work begins. The enquiry button opens your email app.
