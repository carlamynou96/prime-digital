Answers without the jargon

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Explore common questions about websites, Google Ads, domain ownership, payment, email and how we work.

Does Prime Digital guarantee a number-one Google ranking?

No. Paid ad placement and organic search rankings change based on competition, budgets, relevance and other factors. We do not guarantee rankings, clicks, leads or sales.

Does the advertised Google Ads price include actual Google ad spend?

The listed package is a campaign price, but the exact breakdown of media budget, service fees, timing and inclusions must be confirmed in writing before paying.

Are you an official Google Partner?

The website links to an individual Google Ads Search certification. A personal certification does not itself prove official company Google Partner status, so no Partner badge is displayed without additional verification.

Can I choose a website that I own outright?

Yes. Current listed once-off packages are Business Launch (R8,500), Business Pro (R14,900) and Growth Website (R24,900), with project-specific written terms.

What happens if I cancel a managed website?

Hosting and management may end when the managed subscription ends. Domain ownership, data handover and any optional website buyout should be confirmed in your service agreement.

Is a domain included in the monthly management fee?

Domain Care covers ongoing administrative support. Domain purchase and registry renewals can be additional; request the precise total before paying.

Is the domain search live?

No. The domain search prepares a WhatsApp request. Availability must be checked with a domain registrar; entering a name is not a reservation.

Can I have several email addresses?

Yes. The listed Email Start, Business, Team and Pro plans cover different mailbox counts and storage allocations.

Can I pay using PayFast?

PayFast remains pending verification. The current website presents an EFT option and requires manual bank payment confirmation; never rely on a screenshot alone as proof of cleared funds.

Can customers log in to a live portal?

A visual demonstration of a possible portal is available, but production account authentication, support tickets, invoices and subscription management are not yet connected.

Where does the planner submit my information?

The planner opens WhatsApp with your own prepared text. It does not save your information in a website database.

Can you help me transfer an existing domain?

We can discuss DNS connections and registrant-controlled transfers. Any transfer needs appropriate authorisation and depends on the registrar.

What is involved in a website launch?

Typically: agreed scope, business information, design, DNS and email requirements, testing, client review, approvals, then launch.

Are there any fake client testimonials on this site?

No. The portfolio gallery uses explicitly labeled design concepts rather than passing illustrations off as client case studies.
