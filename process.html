How it works

From your very first idea to a confident launch.

No mystery process. Know what we ask for, what we agree on and how a website or advertising campaign moves toward launch.

01

Discovery & goals

We discuss your business, customers, current digital presence and what success would mean.

02

Scope & proposal

We confirm deliverables, budget, third-party services, ownership and timelines before work begins.

03

Information & access

You supply approved content, domain details or ad-account access. Do not send passwords over general chat.

04

Design or setup

We build the agreed pages, settings or campaigns according to the scope.

05

Quality review

You review drafts, links, content, contact points and the necessary approvals.

06

Launch & handover

Only approved projects go live. We clarify recurring maintenance or future support terms.

07

Measure & improve

Use actual data, customer feedback and your business goals to inform improvements.
